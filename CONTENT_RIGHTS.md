## Contributions and corrections

Submit software you are entitled to license under MIT. For new educational material, identify whether it is original or externally sourced, include provenance and applicable permission/license information, and avoid copying commercial question banks or explanations. Do not silently add a new open-content license to an existing item.

If you believe material was included without permission, contact the maintainer privately through the contact address published at [examshortcut.in/contact](https://examshortcut.in/contact/). Identify the file or question ID, the material concerned, and the basis for your rights. The maintainer will review corrections, attribution requests, and removal requests. Do not include private documents or contact details in a public issue.
