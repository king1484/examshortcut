# All-exam content expansion — 22 October 2026

## Published scope

Every one of the 15 portal exams now has an original, sourced preparation or next-stage guide. The new collection adds 30 expandable learning checks and 75 browser-local checklist actions. These are editorial exercises, not recalled questions, official assessment tools or qualification predictions. Existing CGL guides keep their URLs. Fourteen other exams now have guide indexes and detail routes.

- SSC CGL: response-sheet audit, raw-score arithmetic, supported objections and the Tier 2 bridge.
- SSC CHSL: mixed revision and a separate keyboard routine; keep the 2025 result apart from the 2026 schedule.
- SSC MTS: session-aware practice and the difference between MTS and Havaldar next stages.
- SSC GD: CBT shortlist, official PET/PST call letters, category conditions and document readiness.
- SSC CPO: written revision, closed correction window and later physical/post-specific requirements.
- RRB NTPC: graduate versus UG CENs, post choices, additional typing/aptitude requirements and old-key archives.
- RRB Group D: raw-score review, PET instructions and the restricted CCAA modification opportunity.
- RRB ALP/Technician: separate CENs, Technician grades, objection evidence and technical versus shared-subject preparation.
- RRB JE: city slip versus e-call letter, final CBT 1 revision and branch-subject continuity.
- CAT: evidence in VARC, set selection in DILR and QA setup; mock percentiles are not official percentiles.
- IBPS PO: mains-result waiting period, banking briefs, truthful interview examples and documents.
- IBPS Clerk/CSA: current mains structure, DI bases, reasoning constraints and local-language evidence.
- SBI PO: official Phase 3 schedule, interview examples, collaborative group exercises and documents.
- SBI Clerk: regular/special-drive advertisement separation, mains review and language conditions.
- UP Police: final result versus later joining instructions, result evidence and document consistency.

## Notice research and corrections

Official pages checked through Jina Reader, the web tool or the browser on 21–22 October. Some official pages are cached; lack of a new notice in a retrieved page does not prove none can appear later. No result was inferred from a tentative calendar date.

Three notice explainers added: JE CEN 04/2026 city-intimation link (official table dated 17 October and candidate login verified), CPO's 20–28 November window (SSC schedule dated 1 October), and the two detailed NTPC 2026 notifications (graduate 06/2026 and UG 07/2026). NTPC application dates and vacancy totals were not promoted from incompletely inspected full PDFs.

JE's guide/process now acknowledge the published city link rather than describing it only as expected. CPO no longer keeps the closed 8–10 October correction phase active. IBPS PO no longer promotes an expected result month as a current release; the official cycle page had no new Main-result notice in this review. The general process FAQ asks how keys work rather than implying that a future exam's key is already released.

Primary sources include SSC's notice board and 1 October schedule, unified RRB notice tables and linked PDFs, the public JE candidate login, IBPS PO/CSA cycle pages, SBI careers and Phase 3 notice, CAT's official portal and UPPRPB's result notice. The source links remain visible on every new guide and article.

## Search basis and discovery

The verified Search Console domain property is accessible. Its performance report viewed during this work showed CGL shift-specific queries among the strongest visible non-brand queries. This supports continuing accurate shift coverage; it does not establish search volume for the new guides or guarantee their performance. The all-exam expansion is based on distinct candidate tasks and existing subject coverage.

The indexing report was last updated 12 October, before this release. Its discovered/crawled-but-not-indexed groups are a Google processing backlog, not evidence that all excluded URLs should be forced into the sitemap. Intentional noindex, redirects and alternate canonicals remain excluded.

All new guide/detail/index pages enter the canonical sitemap, internal exam links and site search. News also enters RSS. The publication workflow validates evidence, builds, verifies guide coverage and live versions, deploys and submits changed indexable URLs through IndexNow. Submit the regenerated sitemap through the verified Google property after deployment. Submission is a crawl-discovery action; it is not proof of indexing.
