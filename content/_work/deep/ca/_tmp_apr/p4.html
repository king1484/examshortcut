MNRE Achieves 25 GW Renewable Energy Capacity Addition in FY25In April 2025, the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) announced a record-breaking addition of 25 gigawatts (GW) of Renewable Energy (RE) capacity for the Fiscal Year 2024-25 (FY25), marking nearly 35% increase from 18.57 GW in FY24.

  • As of March 2025, the total installed power generation capacity in India was 483.26 GW, marking the highest increase in any year.
  • As of February 28, 2025, India has installed a total of 222.86 GW of non-fossil fuel-based power capacity. This includes 102.57 GW of solar power, 48.59 GW of wind power, 11.45 GW of bio-power, 52.07 GW of hydro power, and 8.18 GW of nuclear power.

i.Solar power drove 80% of the renewable capacity addition, rising from 15 GW (FY24) to 21 GW (FY25), a 38% increase.
ii.Solar module manufacturing capacity doubled from 38 GW (March 2024) to 74 GW (March 2025).

Paradip Port Retained No.1 Spot in Cargo Handling Among Major Ports in FY25According to provisional estimates of the Indian Ports Association (IPA), (Odisha)-based Paradip Port Authority (PPA), formerly Paradip Port Trust, retained the top spot in cargo handling among Indian major ports and has joined the exclusive 150 Million Metric Tonnes (MMT) by handling a cargo of 150.41 MMT in Financial Year 2024-25 (FY25). This marks an increase of 3.5%, compared to 145.4 MMT in FY24.

  • It is closely followed by Kandla (Gujarat)-based Deendayal Port saw an increase of over 13%, from 132.MMT (in FY24) to 150.15 MMT (in FY25) and Mumbai (Maharashtra)-based Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority (JNPA) saw a growth of 7.4% in cargo handled to 86 MMT.

i.The data showed that most of the 12 major Indian Ports recorded growth of 4.35% in cargo traffic for FY25 to 855 MMT compared to 819.30 MMT reported in FY24.
ii.Kutch (Gujarat)-based Mundra Port, India’s biggest commercial port which is owned by Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited (APSEZ), has become the 1st Indian port to handle over 200 MMT of cargo volume in FY25.

  • Mundra Port has registered a growth of 11.5% in cargo handling, to 200.7 MMT (in FY25) over the 180 MMT (in FY24).

It is a statutory body constituted in 1966 under Societies Registrations Act. It aims to foster growth and development of all major ports which are under the supervisory control of Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways (MoPSW).

MoEFCC Report: Over 13,056 sq km of Forest Area Encroached in India; MP LeadsThe Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) has submitted a report to the National Green Tribunal (NGT), revealing that as of March 2024, a total of 13,056 square kilometers (sq km) (or 13,05,668.1 hectares) of forest area across 25 States and Union Territories (UTs) in India was under encroachment.

  • This marks a significant increase compared to the 7,506.48 sq km of encroached forest land reported in April 2023.

State-wise Forest Encroachment Data:
i.According to the report, Madhya Pradesh (MP) has the highest forest encroachment, covering 5,460.9 sq km, followed by Assam with 3,620.9 sq km.
ii.The major states affected are Arunachal Pradesh (AR) recorded 534.9 sq km of forest encroachment, followed by Odisha with 405.07 sq km, Uttar Pradesh (UP) with 264.97 sq km, Mizoram with 247.72 sq km, Jharkhand with 200.40 sq km, and Chhattisgarh with 168.91 sq km.
iii.Other affected states include Tamil Nadu (TN) (157.68 sq km), Andhra Pradesh (AP) (133.18 sq km), Gujarat (130.08 sq km), Punjab (75.67 sq km), Uttarakhand (49.92 sq km), Kerala (49.75 sq km), Tripura (42.42 sq km), Andaman & Nicobar (A&N) Islands (37.42 sq km), and Manipur (32.7 sq km).
iv.10 States and UTs are yet to submit their forest encroachment data are Bihar, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh (HP), Rajasthan, Telangana, West Bengal (WB), Nagaland, Delhi, Jammu and Kashmir (J&K) and Ladakh.
Recorded Forest Area (RFA):
The Forest Area or Recorded Forest Area (RFA) includes land officially designated as forest by the GoI under the Indian Forest Act, 1927, regardless of actual tree cover. It is divided into three categories:

  • Reserved Forests (RF): Fully protected, with restrictions on activities like hunting and grazing.
  • Protected Forests (PF): Some activities are allowed unless explicitly restricted.
  • Unclassed Forests: Areas not classified as reserved or protected.

About Ministry of Environment, Forest, and Climate Change (MoEFCC):
Union Minister – Bhupendra Yadav (Constituency -Alwar, Rajasthan)
Minister of State (MoS) – Kirtivardhan Singh (Constituency- Gonda, Uttar Pradesh UP)

MoR: India Produced 1,681 Locomotives in FY25, Surpasses the USA and EuropeAccording to the Ministry of Railways (MoR), India has emerged as a global leader in railway locomotive manufacturing, with a record production of 1,681 locomotives in the financial year 2024-25 (FY25), marking an increase of 19% (209) locomotives over 1,472 locomotives produced in FY24.

  • With this, India surpasses the total locomotive production of regions like: the United States of America (USA), Europe, South America, Africa and Australia.

Key Points:
i.Significant increase in locomotives production in last one decade: MoR highlighted due to the ‘Make in India’ initiative, the locomotives manufacturing saw sharp increase, with 9,168 locomotives produced between 2014-24, with annual average of nearly 917, compared to 4,695 locomotives produced between 2004-2014, with annual average of 470.
ii.Highest number of locomotives producing units: West Bengal(WB) based Chittaranjan Locomotives Works,CLW(700), Uttar Pradesh(UP) based Banaras Locomotive Works,BLW (477), Punjab based Patiala Locomotive Works,PLW (304) and Bihar based Madhepura unit (100)and Bihar based Marhowrah unit (100).
iii.Majorty of Locomotives produced for freight trains: WAG-9/9H with 1,047 locomotives, topped among the total locomotives produced in FY25; followed by WAP-7 (272 locomotives); WAG-9 Twin (148 locomotives); WAG-12B ( 100 locomotives); WDG 4G/6G (100 locomotives); WAG-9HH (7 locomotives); NRC (5 locomotives) and WAP-5 (2 locomotives).
About Ministry of Railways (MoR):
Union Minister- Ashwinin Vaishnaw (Rajya Sabha member- Odisha)
Minister of State (MoS)- V. Somanna (Constituency- Tumkur, Karnataka); and Ravneet Singh Bittu (Rajya Sabha member- Rajasthan)

IN Signs Rs 270 Crore Deal with Kirloskar for Indigenous Marine Diesel Engine 
On April 2, 2025, the Indian Navy(IN) signed a project sanction order under the Make-I category with Pune (Maharashtra)-based Kirloskar Oil Engines Limited (KOEL) for the design and development of a 6-megawatt (MW) medium-speed marine diesel engine.

  • The agreement, finalized in New Delhi(Delhi) in the presence of Sanjeev Kumar, Secretary of Defence Production(DP), Ministry of Defence(MoD) and Vice Admiral Krishna Swaminathan, Vice Chief of the Naval Staff(VCNS), aims to reduce import dependency in marine propulsion systems.

Key Details of the Project:

  • Funding: Rs 270 crore (70% government-funded) for a prototype engine with over 50% indigenous content.
  • Scope: Includes developing a detailed design for 3–10 MW diesel engines for main propulsion and power generation on Indian Navy and Coast Guard ships.
  • Goal: Replace imports of high-capacity engines from foreign Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs).

BANKING & FINANCE

SBI Research Report: MSMEs in Bihar, UP, Odisha, N-E availing more Mudra loansRecently in April 2025, the State Bank of India (SBI) released a report “The PMMY: A Decade of Audacious Dreams: Bolder, Bigger and Indeed Beautiful” on the ten years of Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY), which stated that the share of MUDRA (Micro Units Development and Refinancing Agency) loans in Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha, and the North-east has grown significantly, indicating a more inclusive credit distribution pattern.
Key Findings:
Distribution of Mudra Accounts:
i.Out of the 52 crore MUDRA accounts that have been opened in the past 10 years, Shishu constitute 78% of them, 20% are Kishor and the remaining 2% are Tarun/Tarun Plus.
ii.The share of Shishu in total has declined from 93% in FY16 to 51.7% in (Financial Year) FY25, while the Kishor account share has increased to 44.7% in FY25 from 5.9% in FY16, indicating the growth of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).
State-wise women entrepreneurs:
i.Among states, Bihar has the largest number of PMMY women entrepreneurs (4.2 crore), followed by Tamil Nadu (4.0 crore) and West Bengal (3.7 crore).
ii.While, Maharashtra has the largest share of women account holders (79%) in total followed by Jharkhand (75%) and West Bengal (73%)
iii.The report has also highlighted that PMMY is an effective tool for women’s empowerment at the grassroots level, as 68% of account holders are women.
iv.Bihar’s share of PMMY loans has risen from 5.67 percent in FY16 to 10.97 percent in FY2. Uttar Pradesh too has witnessed a rise from 9.27 percent to 11.30 percent, while Odisha’s share in PMMY loans has climbed from 4.24 percent to 4.51 percent.
About PMMY:
i.PMMY under the MUDRA was set up by Government of India for development and refinancing activities relating to micro units.
ii.PMMY ensures collateral-free institutional credit up to Rs 20 lakh is provided by Member Lending Institutions (MLIs) i.e. Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs), Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and Micro Finance Institutions (MFIs).
iii. Under the scheme, three categories of interventions have been formulated which include

  • Shishu : Loan up to Rs50,000
  • Kishor : Loan above Rs50,000 and up to Rs5 lakh
  • Tarun : Loan above Rs5 lakh and up to Rs10 lakh
  • Tarun Plus : Loans above Rs10 lakh and up to Rs20 lakh (designed specifically for Tarun category, who have previously availed and successfully repaid loans)

SEBI Extends Retail Algo Trading Deadline to August 1, 2025
In April 2025, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), headquartered in Mumbai (Maharashtra), has extended the deadline for implementing its retail algorithmic (algo) trading regulations from April 1, 2025, to August 1, 2025.

  • The decision follows requests from stock exchanges for additional time to finalize technical standards in collaboration with the Brokers’ Industry Standards Forum (ISF).

Key Details of the Regulatory Framework:

  • Announcement Date: Introduced on February 4, 2025, the norms target retail investors using automated programs for order execution.
  • Scope: Defines roles for brokers (acting as principals) and algo providers (agents using broker-authorized Application Programming Interfaces/APIs).
  • Tracking Mechanism: All algo orders must carry unique identifiers for audit and tracking purposes.

New Damselfly Species ‘Euphaea Wayanadensis’ Discovered in Kerala
In April 2025, a group of scientists have discovered a new species of damselfly named ‘Euphaea Wayanadensis’ in the Wayanad landscape of the Western Ghats, Kerala. The discovery of new species was published in the peer-reviewed journal ENTOMON.

  • This species marks 191st recorded odonate species (including dragonflies and damselflies) found in Kerala and the 223rd one to be documented from the Western Ghats.

i.The newly discovered species belongs to Euphaeidae family and was identified using integrated taxonomy methods.
ii.Initially, the new species features had been misidentified as Euphaea pseudodispar, a species described from Maharashtra.

  • But, later the study found that the new species exhibits various morphological differences from Euphaea pseudodispar like: longer black hind wing patch, wider and uninterrupted humeral and antehumeral stripes in males, among others.

iii.The new species are observed throughout the year except during the month of March and April.
Note: In 2013, a group of researchers 1st observed the distinct morphotype of ‘Euphaea Wayanadensis’ at the Kalindi river, thirunelli in Wayanad district (Kerala).

Raising Day of the Army Medical Corps 2025 – April 3Every year, the Indian Army (IA) observes the Raising Day of the Army Medical Corps (AMC) on April 3 to honor the founding of the AMC in 1764 and commemorate the sacrifices made by medical professionals in the line of duty.

  • April 3, 2025, marked the 261st Raising Day of the AMC, a specialized corps dedicated to providing comprehensive healthcare services to active personnel, veterans, and their families.
  • AMC’s motto, “Sarve Santu Niramaya” (Sanskrit for “Let all be free from disease and disability”), reflects its commitment to holistic health and preventive care.

Historical Evolution of AMC:
i.The origins trace back to the Indian Medical Service (IMS), established in 1612 during the British East India Company’s rule, with John Woodall as the first Surgeon General.
ii.In 1764, medical services were formalized under the Company’s armies.
iii.The administrative headquarters of IAMC was established in Pune (Maharashtra) in May 1943. Post-independence, IAMC was renamed Army Medical Corps (AMC) on 26 January 1950 (Republic Day).
