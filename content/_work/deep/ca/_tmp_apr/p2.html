Waaree Energies opens India’s largest solar cell plant in GujaratIn March 2025, Mumbai (Maharashtra) based Clean energy player Waaree Energies Limited (WEL) has launched India’s largest solar cell manufacturing facility in Chikhli, Gujarat, with a 5.4 Gigawatt (GW) capacity and an investment of Rs 2,500 crore. The plant features advanced Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact (TOPCon) technology, which is known for its high efficiency and improved energy conversion rates.

  • The inauguration event was attended by Union Minister Pralhad Joshi , Ministry of New and Renewable Energy(MNRE) and Gujarat Chief Minister(CM) Bhupendra Patel along with other senior government officials.

About Solar cell manufacturing facility:
i.Spread across 150 acres, with a built-up area of 101 acres, the Chikhli facility underscores Waaree’s dedication to a sustainable energy future for India. The plant also manufactures Mono PERC solar power cells, with trial production commenced in January 2025.
ii.Waaree Energies has invested around USD 350 million (Rs.3,000 crore) in its Chikhli campus, demonstrating its commitment to advancing solar technology and strengthening its presence in the global renewable energy market.

  • This facility is one of four manufacturing plants operated by the company in India, with the others located in Surat (Gujarat), Nandigram (West Bengal, WB), and Noida (Uttar Pradesh, UP).

iii.The facility can produce 20 solar cells per second with 25% efficiency, supporting India’s drive for energy independence and establishing the company as a leader in solar technology.
iv.The new facility’s TOPCon solar cells feature a thin oxide layer and a poly-crystalline silicon layer, forming a highly efficient passivated contact that enhances energy conversion rates.
Note: India’s solar power capacity has surged from 2.82 GW in 2014 to 104 GW in March 2025, marking an impressive growth of 3,580%.

Gujarat CM Inaugurates India’s Largest Chlorotoluene Plant at Dahej

  • Established with an investment of Rs 350 crore, the plant aims to produce value-added chlorine derivatives, reducing import dependency and boosting exports.

i.The newly inaugurated facility has an annual production capacity of 30,000 tonnes and will manufacture critical chemicals like Benzyl Chloride, Benzyl Alcohol, and Benzaldehyde.
ii.These chemicals are widely used in pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, and dyes. The plant will utilize chlorine, a by-product of caustic soda manufacturing, ensuring efficient resource use.
iii.This aligns with India’s goal to enhance self-reliance in specialty chemicals and expand its global market share.
Note: Operational since 1973, GACL is a leading player in the chemical sector with three manufacturing units (two in Dahej, one in Vadodara). It produces over 35 products, including 8 lakh metric tonnes(MT) of caustic soda annually, and holds a monopoly in India for sodium chlorate and hydrazine hydrate.

IAF Participates in Multinational Air Exercise ‘INIOCHOS-25’ in GreeceIn March 2025, the Indian Air Force (IAF) participated in the 12-day biennial multinational air exercise INIOCHOS-25, held from March 31 to April 11, 2025 at Andravida Air Base of Greece. The exercise is hosted by Greece’s Hellenic Air Force, took place in the historic region of Elis.

  • This biennial exercise provides an opportunity to train in planning and executing Combined Air operations, refine tactics in complex air warfare scenarios, and exchange operational best practices.

About INIOCHOS:
i.The Iniochos exercise, named after the ancient Greek word for “charioteer” or “rein-holder,” is an annual multinational air training event that brings air and ground assets from 15 nations, creating realistic combat situations to prepare for modern warfare challenges.
ii.Originating in the late 1980s as a small-scale tactical drill, it has evolved into one of Europe’s most competitive and realistic air combat training platforms.
iii.It helps air forces sharpen their skills, share tactical knowledge, and build stronger military partnerships.
Note: ​The previous edition of Exercise INIOCHOS was held from April 8 to April 18, 2024 in Greece.
About INIOCHOS-25:
i.The IAF’s participation in the exercise, with its Su-30 MKI fighter jets and IL-78 and C-17 combat support aircraft, enhanced its operational preparedness and strengthened India’s strategic ties with friendly nations.
ii.Apart from the Hellenic Air Force and the IAF, other countries taking part include: France (M-2000), Israel (G-550), Italy (Tornado), Montenegro (B-412), Poland (F-16), Qatar (F-15), Slovenia (PC-9), Spain (F-18), United Arab Emirates (UAE) (M-2000/9), the United States of America (USA) (F-16, KC-46, KC-135).
iii.This edition of the exercise has the largest number of fighter jets and helicopters from participating nations and is divided into three phases:

  • Phase 1 (March 24-30, 2025): Deployment of forces.
  • Phase 2 (March 31-April 11, 2025): Main exercise phase, where various air operations will be carried out.
  • Phase 3 (April 11-13, 2025): Redeployment of forces.

iv.During the main phase, the participating air forces will engage in different air operations such as Offensive and Defensive Counter Air Operations, Strategic Air Operations, Air Support for land and maritime missions, Reconnaissance, Combat Search and Rescue, Time-Sensitive Targeting, and protection of high-value airborne assets.
About Greece:
Prime Minister(PM) – Kyriakos Mitsotakis
Capital – Athens
Currency – Euro (EUR)

  • The external debt increased by 0.7% on a quarterly basis, reaching USD 712.7 billion in September 2024. By December 2024, the external debt-to- Gross Domestic Product(GDP) ratio was 19.1%, slightly up from 19% in the previous quarter.

  • Among the non-government sectors, non-financial corporations held the largest share at 36.5%, followed by deposit-taking corporations (excluding the central bank) at 27.8%. The central government accounted for 22.1%, and other financial corporations held 8.7%.

IndusInd Bank Transfers Rs 10,000 Crore Loans to ICICI, Federal Bank via IBPC 
In March 2025, Mumbai (Maharashtra)-based IndusInd Bank Limited has transferred corporate loans worth over Rs 10,000 crore to ICICI Bank Limited(Mumbai-based) and Federal Bank Limited (Kochi, Kerala-based) through Inter-Bank Participation Certificates (IBPC) to bolster liquidity amid concerns over potential deposit outflows linked to an ongoing accounting probe.

  • The IBPC is a money market instrument used by banks to share loans and meet year-end lending targets. In these transactions, the selling bank receives 40% of the loan amount, which helps in managing liquidity and retiring deposits.

Key Details of the Transaction:
i.Mechanism: Loans were transferred via the IBPC market, a short-term instrument (typically 6 months) allowing banks to borrow/lend against loan collateral.
ii.Interest Rate: Acquired loans carry an interest rate of 7.5–8%.
iii.Purpose: IndusInd Bank aims to secure liquidity to offset risks from a Rs 2,000 crore net worth erosion linked to accounting irregularities.

GoI to Acquire 48.99% Stake in Vodafone Idea via Rs 36,950 Crore Equity Conversion
In March 2025, the Government of India (GoI) has approved a proposal to raise its stake in Mumbai (Maharashtra)-based Vodafone Idea Limited (VIL) to 48.99% by converting outstanding spectrum auction dues worth Rs 36,950 crore into equity.

  • This move, part of the September 2021 Reforms and Support Package for Telecom Sector, aims to alleviate financial stress on the debt-laden telecom operator.

i.Under Section 62(4) of the Companies Act, 2013, VIL will issue 3,695 crore equity shares (face value: Rs 10 each) to the GoI. The shares will be issued within 30 days after securing approvals from regulators, including the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
ii.Post-conversion, the GoI will become the single-largest shareholder, surpassing current promoters Aditya Birla Group (14.76%) and Vodafone Group (22.56%).
iii.The conversion addresses VIL’s deferred spectrum liabilities, including dues for the Financial Year 2025-26(FY26) amounting to Rs 32,723.5 crore (covering 2015 spectrum auction shortfalls and adjusted gross revenue (AGR) obligations).
iv.The GoI’s enhanced stake reflects its commitment to sustaining competition in India’s telecom sector.

Russia Unveils ‘Zircon’ Hypersonic Missile to Strengthen Naval DominanceIn March 2025, Russia unveiled the ‘Zircon’ Hypersonic Missile, a cutting-edge weapon capable of reaching speeds of Mach 9 (over 10,000 kilometers per hour, km/h) at altitudes of 20 km. Zircon is described as a “strategic asset” representing a significant leap in military technology, capable of striking land, sea, and airborne targets with unmatched precision and speed.

ii.Its maximum strike distance is estimated to be around 1,500 km (932 miles), allowing long-range strikes, significantly extending Russia’s long-range strike potential.
iii.In March 2025, Russia officially launched “Perm“, a 4th-generation nuclear-powered attack submarine and the first Russian submarine designed to carry and launch hypersonic Zircon missiles.

New Snakehead Fish Species ‘Channa nachi’ Discovered in Meghalaya
In March 2025, a newly discovered species of snakehead fish, “Channa nachi” has been identified in a remote stream in South Garo Hills, Meghalaya, a biodiversity hotspot in Northeast India.

  • It was first collected in 2021 by local fish enthusiast Farham Sangma from a shallow, slow-flowing tributary of the Simsang River system. The species was officially recognized and described in the scientific journal Zootaxa.

i.The species is named “nachi” in honor of Farham Sangma, acknowledging his contribution to the discovery.

  • The term “nachi” is derived from the Garo language, reflecting the indigenous culture’s connection to local biodiversity.

ii.Channa nachi is a newly identified species of snakehead fish, distinguished by its slender body and striking bicolor appearance.
iii.Its coloration transitions from a creamish-yellow front to a deep blue back, complemented by 10–12 pale brown saddles on its dorsal half and pale-blue marbling along its sides.
